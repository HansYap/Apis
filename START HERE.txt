APIS — YOUTUBE TO OBSIDIAN
=========================

Keep the useful parts of a video and connect them in your Obsidian vault.
Apis runs locally. Use your own ChatGPT chat for the AI step; no API key is
needed and Apis never uploads your transcripts to ChatGPT automatically.

FIRST RUN

1. Keep the entire app folder together, including web/, assets/, and
   requirements.txt. Double-click "Start Apis.cmd".
2. On a new computer, Apis offers to install its desktop interface from PyPI
   inside .packages/. This is a one-time setup. Python 3.11+ with Tk and pip,
   and the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, are required on Windows.
3. Click "Connect your vault" at the bottom, or choose your folder in the
   Obsidian screen. Pick the folder you open as an Obsidian vault.
4. Automatic YouTube captions also need Node.js 22+ or Deno 2.3+. The first
   fetch offers to install the free yt-dlp caption reader inside the app.
   Manual transcript import and Markdown saving work without Node or Deno.

EACH VIDEO

1. TRANSCRIPT
   Paste a YouTube link and click Get transcript. You can also paste captions
   or choose Import file to load a UTF-8 TXT, SRT, or VTT file. Expand Video
   details to edit the title or choose a caption language such as en or ms.
   Leave language blank to choose automatically.

2. CHATGPT
   Click Read with ChatGPT. Choose Skim + deep dive, Just the essentials,
   or Follow a question. Add your question or focus, then click
   Save & copy prompt. Open folder to find the saved TXT.
   In your ChatGPT chat, attach that file, paste the prompt, and send it.
   Review important details against their timestamps.

3. OBSIDIAN
   Copy ChatGPT's Markdown into Your note, or use Import Markdown.
   Choose your vault, then click Save & connect.
   Open Obsidian's Graph view or a topic's Backlinks to explore connections.

Choose your vault before preparing the prompt to reuse existing YouTube topic
names. Switching screens keeps your current text. Ctrl+L returns to the link.
New video asks before clearing a transcript or note from the workspace.

WHAT GETS SAVED

  Transcripts/          Timestamped TXT files beside the app
  Vault/YouTube/Notes/  Video notes with source links
  Vault/YouTube/Topics/ Topic notes for new [[wikilinks]]

Links belong in the note itself. For example: "Use [[Active recall]] to test
yourself, then use [[Spaced repetition]] to schedule the next review."
Apis reuses matching vault notes where it can identify one and creates a topic
note otherwise. Saving again creates a numbered copy rather than overwriting.

IF CAPTIONS DO NOT LOAD

Use the three-dot menu beside Import file to open the video or install/update
the caption reader. In YouTube, choose Show transcript, copy the captions,
and paste them into Apis. Some videos have no captions or restrict retrieval.

Captions only cover speech. Add screenshots to your ChatGPT request for charts,
code, demonstrations, or other details that appear only on screen.

DESIGN AND PRIVACY

The dark graphite/violet design uses coral accents, frosted panels, and original
SVG artwork. DM Sans is bundled with its open font license; it loads locally.
There are no remote font requests or analytics. Apis does not read browser
cookies or sign in to YouTube. Settings, transcripts, and dependencies stay out
of Git.
